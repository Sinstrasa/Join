Help

Welcome to the help page for Join, your guide to using our Kanban project management tool. Here, you will find an overview of what Join is, how it works, and how to use its most important features.

What is Join?

Join is a Kanban-based project management tool designed to help you organize tasks, manage projects, and collaborate with others.

Kanban is a method for visualizing work and tracking the progress of individual tasks. In Join, tasks are displayed as cards on a board and organized into different sections depending on their current status.

Join was developed as part of a web development project at the Developer Akademie. It is intended for educational purposes and is not designed for extensive business use.

How to use it

1.

Exploring the Board

When you open the Board, you will see an overview of all your tasks. Tasks are organized into four different sections: To do, In progress, Await feedback, and Done.

This allows you to quickly see the current status of each task.

2.

Creating Contacts

Before assigning tasks, you can add contacts to your project.

Go to the Contacts section and click Add new contact. Enter the contact's name, email address, and phone number.

Once the contact has been created, you can assign them to tasks.

3.

Adding Tasks

To create a new task, go to Add Task or use the add-task button on the board.

Enter the task information, such as:

  • Title
  • Description
  • Due date
  • Priority
  • Category
  • Assigned contacts
  • Subtasks

After entering the required information, click Create Task to add the task to your board.

4.

Moving Tasks

Tasks can be moved between the different sections of the board.

Move a task from To do to In progress when you start working on it. If the task requires feedback, move it to Await feedback. Once the task is completed, move it to Done.

This makes it easy to keep track of the progress of your project.

5.

Editing Tasks

Click on a task to open its details.

From there, you can edit information such as the title, description, due date, priority, assigned contacts, and subtasks.

Save your changes when you are finished.

6.

Deleting Tasks

Open the task you want to remove and select Delete.

Be careful when deleting tasks, as they will be permanently removed from the board.

7.

Using Subtasks

Subtasks allow you to divide a larger task into smaller steps.

When a subtask is completed, you can mark it as done. The task will show your progress so you can easily see how much work is still left.

8.

Searching for Tasks

Use the search field on the Board to quickly find a specific task.

Enter the task title or a keyword. Join will display the tasks that match your search.

Need more help?

If something does not work as expected, try refreshing the page or logging in again.

When creating or editing tasks and contacts, make sure that all required fields are filled in correctly.
